\begin{frame}{Takeaway}
\begin{itemize}
  \item For the 9 urbanization tasks, a \textbf{single view is enough}: street-view alone matches or
        beats the two-view setup (full$-$best1 $\leq 0$) for every strategy and every task.
  \item \textbf{Adding reasoning does not fix it}: 16k-token thinking and a 4-turn observe$\to$reason$\to$
        self-check$\to$commit conversation raise accuracy, but the single view rises with it.
  \item Across all 4 models vision \emph{does} help (full $>$ blind) --- but it is one-view vision.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
