\begin{frame}{Setup \& what each split tests}
\small
Two LoRA-trained \textbf{Qwen3.5-9B} models, each evaluated on \textbf{its own validation split}
and on the \textbf{shared benchmark}. Four image modes: \textbf{blind} (no images),
\textbf{sv} (street-view only), \textbf{sat} (marked satellite only), \textbf{full} (both).\\[6pt]
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{per-city val} --- \emph{unseen locations within cities the model trained on.}
  Tests in-distribution generalisation. ($n=6{,}984$)
\item \textbf{seen/unseen val} --- \emph{entirely held-out cities the model has \textbf{never seen at all}.}
  The hard test: geographic generalisation to brand-new cities. ($n=8{,}831$)
\item \textbf{benchmark} --- a stratified \textbf{10\% of locations from \emph{every} city}
  (\texttt{BENCHMARK\_RATIO=0.10}, location-level), extracted \emph{before} either split, so it is
  identical and held-out for both models. ($n=5{,}240$)
\end{itemize}
\textbf{Fusion} $=$ full $-\max(\text{sv},\text{sat})$. All: thinking off, greedy, edge 512 (matches training). Bold sv/sat $=$ better single view.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{How to read fusion on these 9 tasks}
\small
The 9 urban-attribute tasks are, \textbf{by construction, answerable from a single view} ---
land use, building height, road type, etc.\ are each readable from \emph{either} the satellite
or the street-view alone. So we \textbf{expect} \texttt{full} $\approx$ the better single view, and
fusion $\approx 0$ here is the \textbf{designed behaviour, not a model deficiency}.\\[6pt]
What these tables actually show:
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{full never \emph{loses}} to the better single view (fusion $\gtrsim 0$ throughout) --- adding
  the second view doesn't confuse the model.
\item Where one view is uninformative, full correctly tracks the \textbf{informative} one
  (bolded sv/sat per row).
\item The \textbf{mismatch\_*} tasks (appendix) are \textbf{literally unanswerable from one view}:
  the other view \emph{is the answer choices}. There, fusion is +40--70 --- the mechanism works
  whenever the task actually forces it.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{External RS-VLM baselines --- GeoChat \& SkySenseGPT (zero-shot)}
\small
Two off-the-shelf remote-sensing VLMs (LLaVA-1.5-7B forks, CLIP-ViT-L/14 @ 504px)
evaluated on the \textbf{shared benchmark}, \textbf{satellite-only}, \textbf{zero-shot}.
They are \textbf{single-image, overhead-only} --- so they see \emph{only} the marked
satellite tile (our \texttt{sat} mode), never street-view, on all 9 Family-1 tasks
($n=3{,}135$).\\[4pt]
\begin{itemize}
\item They \textbf{never trained on the red-dot marker} nor on our attribute taxonomies
  --- this is a pure out-of-distribution transfer probe.
\item Fair column to compare against is \textbf{our trained \texttt{sat}} on the same
  benchmark (single satellite view, like-for-like input).
\end{itemize}
All: greedy, \texttt{max\_new\_tokens}=8, 0\% unparseable. SkySenseGPT $>$ GeoChat overall.
\end{frame}
