\begin{frame}{Setup}
\small
Two LoRA-trained \textbf{Qwen3.5-9B} models (merged bf16, epoch-4), each run on the held-out
\textbf{benchmark} and its \textbf{own validation split}. Four image modes:
\textbf{blind} (no images), \textbf{sv} (street-view only), \textbf{sat} (marked satellite only),
\textbf{full} (both).\\[4pt]
\textbf{Fusion} $=$ full $-\max(\text{sv},\text{sat})$: accuracy gained from \emph{both} views vs.\ the
better single view. \textcolor{good}{Positive} $\Rightarrow$ genuine fusion; $\approx 0 \Rightarrow$ leans on one view.\\[4pt]
\emph{per-city} $=$ cities seen in training; \emph{seen/unseen} $=$ held-out cities.
All: thinking off, greedy, image edge 512 (matches training). Bold sv/sat $=$ better single view.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Verdict (the 9 tasks)}
\textbf{Fusion is $\approx 0$ on the urban attributes.} \texttt{full} $\approx$ the better single view ---
the model does \textbf{not} meaningfully combine satellite + street-view; it rides whichever single
view is stronger per task.\\[6pt]
Most ``fusion-like'': \textbf{transit\_density} (+3.6 to +4.5) and \textbf{urban\_density} (+2.4 to +3.1) --- modest.\\[6pt]
\textcolor{bad}{Caveat:} \textbf{green\_space} is leaked --- 100\% even in \textbf{blind} mode (answered from option
text, no vision). Excluded from the fusion mean above.
\end{frame}
