\begin{frame}{What this compares}
\small
Four off-the-shelf \textbf{remote-sensing VLMs}, evaluated \textbf{zero-shot} on the
\textbf{shared benchmark}, \textbf{satellite-only}, against \textbf{our fine-tuned
Qwen3.5-9B}.
\begin{itemize}
\item All five see the \textbf{same single marked satellite tile} (our \texttt{sat} mode),
  the \textbf{same 9 Family-1 tasks}, the \textbf{same 3{,}135 questions} --- like-for-like input.
\item The four externals are \textbf{single-image, overhead-only} and \textbf{never trained}
  on EOLLM, on our 4-way MCQ format, or on the red-dot marker our tiles carry
  $\Rightarrow$ pure out-of-distribution transfer.
\item Fair comparison column $=$ \textbf{our trained \texttt{sat}} (single satellite view,
  identical input modality).
\end{itemize}
\vspace{4pt}
\footnotesize All: greedy, \texttt{max\_new\_tokens}=8, single GPU (RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell), batched.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Reading the externals}
\small
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{EarthDial-4B-RGB is the strongest zero-shot RS-VLM} (41.3) --- and the
  smallest (4B) and fastest. Its edge is \textbf{layout/structure} (road type, junction
  type, green space, urban density), where high-res InternVL tiling resolves geometry the
  504px single-crop LLaVA models miss.
\item \textbf{SkySenseGPT $>$ GeoChat} throughout; both LLaVA models keep \textbf{road
  surface} (pure texture, view-agnostic) but collapse toward chance on count/context tasks.
\item All externals are at/near 0.25 chance on \textbf{amenity richness} --- counting
  services from one overhead tile is genuinely hard.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

% ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
\begin{frame}{\large Caveat --- don't over-read LHRS / EarthDial}
\small
\textbf{LHRS-Bot-Nova} and \textbf{EarthDial} share \textbf{OSM provenance} with our labels:
\begin{itemize}
\item LHRS-Bot pretrained on $\sim$1M \textbf{OSM-aligned} RS image--text pairs (thousands of cities).
\item EarthDial's instruction QA is itself \textbf{OSM-derived}.
\end{itemize}
Our Family-1 labels are OSM-grounded, so their scores may reflect \textbf{shared OSM priors}
(and possible image overlap), not pure visual skill --- treat as an \textbf{upper bound}.
GeoChat/SkySenseGPT do \emph{not} share this provenance.\\[6pt]
\textbf{Bottom line:} even granting that upper bound, the best external (EarthDial 41.3)
still trails our trained \texttt{sat} (54.9) by \textbf{13.6 pts} on the identical single
satellite view --- and our model wins all 9 tasks.
\end{frame}
