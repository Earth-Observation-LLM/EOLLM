\begin{frame}{The question}
\large
Each urban VQA question shows \textbf{4 street-view cameras}
(\texttt{fwd}, \texttt{bwd}, \texttt{cross\_left}, \texttt{cross\_right}).
\vspace{6pt}
\begin{block}{Hypothesis}
The full 360\textdegree{} view is \textbf{redundant}: the \textbf{one image the model
attends to most} keeps the answer correct --- and that image is the
\textbf{forward camera}.
\end{block}
\vspace{6pt}
\footnotesize
\textbf{Method.} Solve each question on all 4 images $\to$ keep only the $k$ most-attended
$\to$ re-ask greedily. \textbf{Survival \%} $=$ fraction of solved questions still correct
on the kept subset.
\par\vspace{3pt}
\textbf{Arms:} \textcolor{ours}{\textbf{top}} (most-attended) $\,\vert\,$
\textcolor{fwdc}{\textbf{fwd}} (always forward) $\,\vert\,$
random (pooled over 3 seeds) $\,\vert\,$
\textcolor{botc}{\textbf{bottom}} (least-attended).
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Take-home}
\large
\begin{itemize}
\item[\textcolor{good}{\ding{51}}] The 360\textdegree{} view is \textbf{redundant} ---
 \textbf{one} street-view image gives $89.5\%$ survival, and that image is the
 \textbf{forward camera} (attention's \#1 in $94.8\%$).
\item[\textcolor{good}{\ding{51}}] The right image \textbf{beats a random one} by
 \textbf{+4.4 pp} (CI $[2.6,6.1]$) overall, \textbf{+8.8 pp} on image-dependent
 questions.
\item[\textcolor{bad}{\ding{55}}] Caveat: several topics carry \textbf{class-prior
 leakage}; on leakage-safe topics alone the raw gap touches 0. The
 \textbf{image-dependent} subset carries the claim ($+8.8$ pp).
\end{itemize}
\vspace{8pt}
\normalsize\centering\footnotesize
$3{-}4{\times}$ fewer image tokens per question, same answer at $k{=}1$.\\[2pt]

\end{frame}
