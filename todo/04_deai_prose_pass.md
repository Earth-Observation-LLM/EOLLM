## Target patterns (from Critic B, hunt and rewrite)
- "is the crux of the paper" / "the picture that emerges" / "sharpens the picture"
  / "makes ... concrete" — meta-narration. Delete.
- "not X, nor Y. It is Z" / "not a claim that ... but ..." — keep AT MOST one.
- Triadic em-dash glosses ("what land use is here, how tall ..., is the road paved")
  on both sides of a parallel — break the symmetry, cut one triad.
- Repeated coined phrase "a single view's worth of ..." (was used 3×) — once max.
- Em-dash overuse as primary clause separator (abstract, intro, discussion) —
  convert ~half to periods/commas.
- "we argue" hedging about our own writing — cut.
- Rhetorical-question-as-forward-reference (3×) — vary.

## Method
Read each section aloud-style; flatten to plain scientific register. Keep the
content and numbers identical — only the cadence changes. Re-grep for the patterns
above after editing.
