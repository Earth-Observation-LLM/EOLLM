\begin{frame}{What changed, what did not}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{What fine-tuning fixed:} every image mode jumps $\sim$14 pts (full $48.6\!\to\!62.5$),
        and the model now clearly \emph{uses} an image --- full beats blind by \textbf{+17.3} pts (raw: $+7.7$).
  \item \textbf{What it did \emph{not} fix:} the two-view advantage. Trained full $-$ best-single-view $=\mathbf{+0.5}$ pt
        overall; on 5 of 9 tasks the best single view still ties or beats both views.
  \item \textbf{Conclusion:} the benchmark's questions are answerable from \emph{either} the satellite or the
        street view alone. Supervised fine-tuning raises the ceiling but the second view contributes almost nothing ---
        the same one-view story as the raw model, now at higher accuracy.
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}
